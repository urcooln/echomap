Add a Teacher Resource Center
What & Why
Add a Teacher Resource Center within the Teacher Portal Resources section that provides classroom-ready communication supports, implementation guides, and professional learning resources. The goal is to help teachers understand, support, and respond to communication differences throughout the school day while maintaining consistency across home, school, and therapy.
Done looks like
Teachers have access to a dedicated Resource Center within their portal.
Resources are organized into practical categories:
Understanding Gestalt Language Processing
Classroom Communication Supports
Regulation & Sensory Supports
AAC Support Strategies
Building Peer Connections
Supporting Transitions & Routines
Language Modeling Examples
Frequently Asked Questions
Each resource can be opened and read directly inside ChildLed.
Resources support bookmarking and completion tracking.
Teachers can quickly save favorite resources for later reference.
Resources are child-safe and educational, not clinical records.
Resources are available only to authorized teacher accounts.
Completion and bookmark status persist across devices and sessions.
Resources can include PDFs, videos, checklists, templates, and classroom handouts.
