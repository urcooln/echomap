Post-Recording Workflow Redesign: Remove Calibration and Switch to Utterance-First Review

Temporarily hide the clinician voice calibration feature from all user-facing workflows.

Reason

Current investigation confirmed that calibration recordings are stored but are not actively used for:

* Speaker diarization
* Speaker attribution
* Clinician-role identification
* Transcript speaker matching
* Phrase extraction
* Gestalt analysis

Because calibration is not affecting outcomes today, it should be hidden until operational speaker matching is implemented. Do not delete existing calibration infrastructure; simply remove it from the clinician workflow and UI.

⸻

New Core Principle

A successful recording should always produce a consistent review workflow.

Clinicians should never be blocked from reviewing language because speaker attribution failed.

⸻

Replace Phrase-First Review With Utterance-First Review

Current (remove)

Transcript → Phrase Candidates → Review Phrases

New Workflow

Transcript → Child Utterance Review → Child Phrase Candidates → Dictionary / Insights

⸻

Step 1: Potential Child Utterances

After transcription completes, automatically generate a review queue of utterances.

Display:

Potential Child Utterances

1. “Let’s blast off!”
2. “Can I close this Word document?”
3. “I need help.”
4. “Look at that!”

For each utterance provide:

* Child
* Not Child
* Unsure

The clinician should be able to move through utterances rapidly using keyboard shortcuts and mobile-friendly controls.

⸻

Step 2: Build Child Language Set

After utterances are reviewed:

Create a confirmed child-language pool consisting only of utterances marked:

* Child

Exclude:

* Not Child
* Unsure

from phrase extraction and reporting.

⸻

Step 3: Generate Child Phrase Candidates

Automatically generate phrase candidates only from confirmed child utterances.

Example:

Child Phrase Candidates

Observed 5 times

“Let’s blast off!”

Observed 3 times

“I need help.”

Observed 2 times

“Look at that!”

⸻

Step 4: Clinical Review

For each phrase candidate:

* Add to Dictionary
* Edit Phrase
* Ignore Candidate
* Mark for Later Review

Display:

* Occurrence count
* First observed date
* Most recent date
* Example utterances

⸻

Speaker Attribution Failure Handling

If automatic speaker grouping fails:

Do NOT block review.

Show:

Session Review Ready

Transcript completed successfully.

Speaker identification was unavailable for this recording.

You can continue reviewing potential child utterances below.

[Retry Speaker Identification]

The review workflow should continue normally.

⸻

Expected Benefits

* Consistent workflow regardless of diarization success.
* Clinicians identify child language before phrase review.
* Reduced review burden.
* Higher phrase accuracy.
* Better alignment with clinical reasoning.
* Removes dependence on calibration and speaker grouping for productive session review.

⸻

This redesign should become the standard post-recording workflow for all recordings until reliable speaker attribution is available.
