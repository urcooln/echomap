Proposed Feature: AAC Device & Vocabulary System Profile
What & Why
AAC vocabulary systems significantly influence language organization, modeling strategies, communication-partner coaching, vocabulary recommendations, and AAC planning. Parents, teachers, paraprofessionals, and clinicians often know the device but not the language system being used.
Add structured AAC information to the Child Profile so all authorized team members can quickly identify both the AAC platform and vocabulary system.
