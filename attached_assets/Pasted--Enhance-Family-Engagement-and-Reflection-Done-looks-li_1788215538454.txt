## Enhance Family Engagement and Reflection

### Done looks like

- Each learning module includes a brief "Try This at Home" section containing practical, non-prescriptive ideas derived from the handbook content.
- Each module includes a "What We Noticed" reflection area where family members may optionally save private observations related to the module.
- Family reflections remain separate from clinical records and are not visible to clinicians unless explicitly shared through existing observation workflows.
- The Learning Center displays estimated reading time for each module and a progress indicator showing percentage completed across the handbook.
- Parents can return to their last-viewed module from the Learning Center home page.
- Modules support accessible reading features including adjustable text size, clear section navigation, and mobile-friendly layouts.

### Family Safety Requirements

- Reflection content is not analyzed for diagnosis, NLA stage, treatment recommendations, or automated clinical interpretation.
- Learning Center content remains educational and informational even when family observations are present.
- Completion status, bookmarks, and reflections are isolated per authenticated family account and never shared across unrelated family members without existing authorization.

### Future-Proofing

- Store module progress, bookmarks, and reflections separately from clinical records so future parent-learning resources can reuse the same infrastructure.
- Support future resource collections without requiring changes to the Parent Coaching Handbook implementation.
- Preserve stable module identifiers and content-version tracking so progress remains consistent when handbook content is updated.

### Clinical Value

The Learning Center should help families move from:

"I don't understand why my child says this."

to

"I have tools to observe, understand, and support communication."

The goal is to increase confidence, understanding, and collaboration across home, school, and therapy while keeping educational content clearly separate from clinical decision-making and medical records.
