Add Session Review Progress Tracker
What & Why
Clinicians need immediate visibility into how much transcript review work remains after a recording is processed. The Child Language Review workflow should provide clear progress indicators so clinicians can quickly understand review status, estimate remaining effort, and confidently pause and resume sessions.
Done looks like
A persistent Review Progress card appears at the top of the Child Language Review page.
The progress card is visible immediately after transcription completes and remains visible throughout review.
The card displays:
Child: X
Not Child: X
Unsure: X
Unintelligible: X
Remaining: X
A visual progress bar shows:
Reviewed utterances
Total reviewable utterances
Percent complete
Counts update immediately after each clinician decision.
Progress persists across refreshes, logout, browser sessions, and device changes.
Progress restores correctly when a clinician returns to a partially reviewed session.
Remaining count always reflects unresolved utterances only.
Progress is calculated from clinician decisions, not AI suggestions or speaker-grouping results.
Speaker-grouping failures do not affect progress calculations.
The component is responsive and remains usable on desktop and mobile devices.

UX Requirements
Place the Review Progress card directly beneath the page title and above the first review item.
Make progress visible without scrolling.
Use positive completion language:
"18 of 42 reviewed"
"24 remaining"
Avoid presenting unresolved items as errors.
Allow clinicians to safely stop and resume review at any time.
Out of scope
Automatic utterance classification.
Clinical interpretation.
NLA stage assignment.
Dictionary updates.
Session note generation.
Success Criteria
Clinicians can answer these questions within one second of opening the review page:
How many utterances have I reviewed?
How many are left?
How much of the session is complete?
Can I safely leave and come back later?
I would also add a small completion message when the review reaches 100%:
Review Complete
All utterances have been reviewed. Continue to Child Phrase Inbox → Session Summary.
