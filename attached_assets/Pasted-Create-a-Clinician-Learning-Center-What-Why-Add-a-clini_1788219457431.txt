Create a Clinician Learning Center
What & Why
Add a clinician-facing Learning Center that serves as EchoMap's professional development and implementation hub. Unlike the Parent Learning Center, this workspace should support clinical decision-making, language-sample interpretation, NLA-informed observation, AAC planning, communication-partner coaching, and EchoMap workflow training.
The goal is to help clinicians consistently apply EchoMap's methodology while maintaining clear boundaries between educational resources and clinical judgment.
Done Looks Like
A new Clinician Learning Center appears under Resources for authorized clinicians.
Learning Center content is organized into structured modules.
Modules can contain text, examples, clinical notes, downloadable resources, videos, reference guides, and implementation checklists.
Clinicians can bookmark modules and mark them complete.
Progress persists across devices and sessions.
Resources are searchable by topic.

Safety Requirements
Educational content only.
No automatic diagnostic recommendations.
No automatic stage assignment.
No treatment recommendations generated from resources.
Resources never modify child records.
Resources remain separate from clinical documentation.
Nice-to-Have Enhancements
"Referenced During Review" links from utterance review screens.
Context-sensitive help icons.
Searchable examples library.
Downloadable clinician handouts.
Case examples showing how reviewed language moves through EchoMap.
Vision
The Clinician Learning Center should function as an integrated professional handbook that teaches clinicians how to interpret and use EchoMap data, while keeping clinical decisions firmly under clinician control.
