EchoMap Language Journey Timeline
Create an interactive "Language Journey Timeline" page that visualizes a child's communication development across sessions, weeks, months, and years.
The purpose is to help clinicians, caregivers, and educational teams understand how language patterns evolve over time.

The timeline should be one of EchoMap's signature features and prioritize clinical meaning, not just data storage.
