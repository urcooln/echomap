Teacher Portal Enhancement
Enhance the Teacher Portal to support meaningful collaboration between teachers and the clinical team while maintaining a simple, classroom-friendly user experience.
The Teacher Portal should focus on helping teachers understand the student's communication, contribute observations, and support carryover across environments.

Goal
The Teacher Portal should function as a quick classroom communication guide and observation tool that helps teachers understand the student's language while contributing valuable real-world data to the child's communication profile.
