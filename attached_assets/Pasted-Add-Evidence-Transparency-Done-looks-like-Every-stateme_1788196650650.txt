Add: Evidence Transparency
Done looks like
Every statement in the generated Session Summary can be traced back to reviewed evidence.
Clinicians can expand a summary section to view the specific confirmed Child utterances, phrase candidates, or dictionary entries that contributed to that statement.
Generated observations clearly distinguish:
Directly observed language
Longitudinal comparisons
AI-generated interpretations
Interpretation sections display a visible label:
AI-generated clinical observation
Clinician review required
Why
Clinicians need to understand why EchoMap generated an observation rather than treating it as a black box.
Add: Communication Growth Snapshot
Done looks like
The Session Summary includes a dedicated section:
Communication Growth Snapshot
When supported by reviewed evidence, summarize:
New phrases observed this session
Repeated phrases observed across sessions
New communication functions observed
Previously documented phrases reused in new contexts
Emerging flexibility or recombination
Increased frequency of meaningful communication
All comparisons use cautious language such as:
"A previously undocumented phrase was observed."
"This phrase has now been observed across multiple sessions."
"Language may be becoming more flexible across contexts."
Why
This is often the first thing clinicians want to know after a session:
What was new?
What changed?
What should I pay attention to?
Add: Family & Team Shareable Highlights
Done looks like
Generate a separate optional section:
Family & Team Highlights
Provide 3–5 plain-language observations suitable for sharing with caregivers or educational teams.
Examples:
"The child used several phrases to request help during the session."
"A new phrase was observed that had not been documented previously."
"The child repeatedly used language to share attention with others."
This section:
Uses non-clinical language
Avoids NLA terminology
Avoids diagnostic language
Requires clinician review before sharing
Never automatically sends to anyone
Why
SLPs frequently need a quick summary they can adapt for parents and teachers.

Add: Session-to-Session Continuity
Done looks like
When previous reviewed sessions exist, EchoMap generates:
Since Last Reviewed Session
Newly observed phrases
Phrases not observed this session
Phrases increasing in frequency
Phrases decreasing in frequency
Communication functions appearing for the first time
Use cautious wording and only reviewed evidence.
Why
This aligns directly with the broader EchoMap goal:
Turning communication into shared understanding across home, school, and therapy.
The most valuable thing EchoMap can tell a clinician isn't just what happened today—it's how today's communication compares with what has been happening over time.
