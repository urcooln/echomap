Voice Calibration Redesign

Redesign the speaker-identification calibration workflow to use a standardized, natural voice sample that can be reused across future sessions.

Calibration Screen

Replace the current scripted prompt:

“I am Clinician. Today is Tuesday, August 25, 2026.”

with:

Create Voice Profile

Please read the sentence below aloud:

“Hello, this is my EchoMap voice sample. My name is [Clinician Name], and I am beginning today’s session.”

This phrase should automatically populate the clinician’s name from their account profile.

Recording Requirements

* Require 5–10 seconds of speech.
* Display a visible recording timer.
* Provide clear start/stop controls.
* Allow re-recording if desired.

Voice Profile Quality Assessment

After recording, automatically evaluate:

* Recording length
* Audio clarity
* Background noise
* Voice consistency
* Signal quality

Display one of:

🟢 Excellent Voice Profile

* High confidence for speaker identification

🟢 Good Voice Profile

* Suitable for speaker identification

🟡 Fair Voice Profile

* Speaker identification may be less accurate

🔴 Poor Voice Profile

* Please re-record in a quieter environment

Success State

Display:

✅ Voice Profile Saved

Your voice profile will be used to help EchoMap automatically distinguish clinician speech from child speech during recordings.

Persistence

* Save the voice profile to the clinician account.
* Do not require calibration before every session.
* Reuse the saved profile across future recordings.
* Allow clinicians to update or replace their voice profile at any time from Settings.

Session Workflow

When recording a session:

1. Detect whether a saved clinician voice profile exists.
2. If a profile exists:
    * Automatically attempt speaker identification.
    * Label matching speech as “Clinician.”
    * Label non-matching speech as “Possible Child Speech.”
3. Display confidence scores for speaker assignments.
4. Allow clinicians to quickly correct speaker labels when needed.
5. Learn from clinician corrections over time to improve future accuracy.

Error Handling

Do not display generic errors.

If calibration fails, specify exactly why:

* Microphone permission denied
* Recording too short
* Recording too quiet
* Excessive background noise
* Upload failure
* Speaker model processing error

Provide actionable instructions for resolution.

Design Goal

The experience should feel professional, intuitive, and trustworthy. Clinicians should complete voice setup once and gain meaningful improvement in automatic speaker separation without repeated calibration at each session.
