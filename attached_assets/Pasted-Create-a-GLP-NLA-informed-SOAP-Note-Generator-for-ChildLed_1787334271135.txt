Create a GLP/NLA-informed SOAP Note Generator for ChildLed.

After session transcription and speaker assignment are complete, automatically generate a draft SOAP note using only utterances assigned to the child.

The SOAP note should include:

SUBJECTIVE

Summarize caregiver, teacher, or clinician reports.

Examples:

* Parent reports increased scripting during transitions.
* Teacher reports reduced frustration during group activities.
* Child appeared excited during preferred activities.

OBJECTIVE

Include objective session data.

Display:

* Session duration
* Number of child utterances
* Number of unique gestalts
* Number of repeated gestalts
* Most frequently used gestalts
* New gestalts identified this session
* Emerging mitigations or recombinations
* Communication functions observed

Example:

Observed gestalts:
• “We have a cat in our bed” (5 occurrences)
• “Let’s go to the moon” (3 occurrences)

New gestalts:
• “Nobody likes broccoli”

Communication functions:
• Requesting
• Protesting
• Commenting
• Self-regulation
• Seeking connection

ASSESSMENT

Generate a clinician-facing interpretation section.

This section must be clearly labeled:

“DRAFT CLINICAL INSIGHTS - REVIEW REQUIRED”

Provide observations such as:

* Frequently repeated gestalts
* Changes in frequency compared to previous sessions
* Emerging mitigations
* Emerging flexible language
* New communication functions
* Gestalts associated with emotional regulation
* Patterns across environments

Examples:

• Child used 3 previously documented gestalts associated with frustration.
• Reduced reliance on highly repetitive gestalts compared to prior sessions.
• Emerging mitigations observed in 4 utterances.
• Increased spontaneous language flexibility noted.

NLA STAGE OBSERVATIONS

Do NOT diagnose or assign a definitive stage.

Instead provide:

“Observed indicators may be consistent with:”

* Predominantly Stage 1 features
* Emerging Stage 2 mitigations
* Emerging Stage 3 recombinations
* Mixed profile

Display confidence levels.

Example:

Observed indicators:
• Stage 1 patterns: High
• Stage 2 patterns: Moderate
• Stage 3 patterns: Low

GESTALT TRACKING

For each recurring gestalt display:

Phrase:
Occurrences this session:
Lifetime occurrences:
Associated contexts:
Reported meaning:
Last observed:

Example:

“We have a cat in our bed”
Session: 5
Lifetime: 42
Meaning: Wants to leave
Contexts: Overstimulation, transitions
Last observed: 8/21/2026

PLAN

Generate clinician-editable recommendations.

Examples:

* Continue tracking newly observed gestalts.
* Confirm suspected meanings with caregiver.
* Monitor emerging mitigations.
* Expand opportunities for self-generated combinations.
* Share updated gestalt dictionary with educational team.

COPY FEATURE

Add:
“Copy SOAP Note”

One-click copy to clipboard.

EXPORT FEATURE

Add:

* Export PDF
* Export to EMR text format
* Export caregiver summary

DISCLAIMER

Display:

“AI-generated draft for clinician review only. ChildLed does not provide diagnoses, clinical decisions, or treatment recommendations.”
