ChildLed SLP Command Center Redesign Prompt
Redesign the current ChildLed clinician home experience into a modern, highly efficient SLP Command Center that functions as a caseload management workspace rather than a simple dashboard.
The primary goal is to help clinicians immediately answer:
Which children need my attention today?
What changed since I last logged in?
What communication insights are emerging?
What actions should I take next?
The design should feel similar to a professional clinical command center, combining the clarity of an EMR dashboard with the usability of modern SaaS products.

Core Design Principle
Every screen should answer:
"What changed, who needs attention, and what should I do next?"
The clinician should be able to identify priorities within 5 seconds of opening the app.
This redesign should make ChildLed feel less like a phrase tracker and more like a communication intelligence platform for managing an entire caseload.
